Garden of the Self is a mobile wellness app that helps people build healthier habits and stronger character through virtue-based journaling, guided daily quests, and measurable personal growth over time. Designed for mission-driven organisations, it turns reflection into an engaging, repeatable practice that can support youth development, mental well-being, and community resilience at scale. By combining accessible mobile technology with evidence-aligned behaviour formation patterns, Garden of the Self offers nonprofits a practical, low-friction tool to extend their impact and provide communities with daily support for purposeful, values-centred growth. Currently Garden of the Self is built around 13 character virtues: Courage, Temperance, Patience, Kindness, Proper Ambition, Modesty, Empathy, Resilience, Curiosity, Respectfulness, Tolerance, Collaboration, and Discipline. After some improvements from St' James Settlement's feedback, this app can be used as a long term tool to develop the character of St James Settlement's community. I believe it will help make the values that St' James Settlement teaches not just a lesson or an aspect of life, but the values that someone lives behind day to day.
