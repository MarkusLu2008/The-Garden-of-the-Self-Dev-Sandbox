Garden of the Self is a cross-platform mobile wellness app that helps people build healthier habits and stronger character through virtue-based journaling, guided daily quests, and measurable personal growth over time. Designed for mission-driven organizations, it turns reflection into an engaging, repeatable practice that can support youth development, mental well-being, and community resilience at scale. By combining accessible mobile technology with evidence-aligned behavior formation patterns, Garden of the Self offers nonprofits a practical, low-friction tool to extend their impact and provide communities with daily support for purposeful, values-centered growth. 

This project is about helping people feel stronger on the inside, one day at a time. It gives users simple daily prompts and reflection tools that encourage kindness, resilience, and healthy habits, so growth feels achievable instead of overwhelming. The goal is to procide a caring, practical way to help people build confidence, connection, and long-term well-being.
